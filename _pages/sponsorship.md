Plainview Old Bethpage Soccer Club is a 501(c)3 Non-Profit Corporation. POBSC has been in existence since 1974 serving the Plainview Old Bethpage area as well as accepting children from all over Long Island. More children and families will utilize our facilities than any other youth sporting organization in the POB area. This year we anticipate registering 1,450+ children in our soccer programs, with almost 40 competitive travel teams.

Your support allows us to continually improve our soccer programs & facilities, provide scholarships for those children who need financial assistance, offer several scholarships to graduating seniors as well as help offset the cost of participation.

Sponsors are vital to our economic health and are one of the reasons our fees are as low as they can be. In the past some of these donations have helped pave the way to allowing POBSC to purchase AED's for every field that our children play on. All of this is made possible with tax-deductible donations from corporations and people like you.

What a wonderful way to invest in the children of our community and the children thank you! Please join in partnership with the fastest growing sport in America!
